Morph operations applies to frames 1–64

tilt

Broadband spectral slope. Raises or lowers every harmonic in proportion to its number.

odd / even

Suppresses even harmonics toward a hollow, square-ish character, or restores them.

formant

A localised peak, not a broadband change. Moves a resonant bump up or down the series.

gate

Removes the quietest harmonics. Thins the spectrum toward something glassier.

interpolate

Replaces the range with a smooth path between its two end frames. Removes jitter.

drive

Adds harmonics by waveshaping each frame. The usual route to growl and grit.

Audition

Audio is off.

Hold a note and press Sweep. A parked position only ever sounds one frame — the other 63 are silent until something moves.
